The Little Book of Stamp Rhymes

Every parcel that leaves our shop carries a four-line rhyme about the stamp inside. The best forty-two are now a small free book, one verse to a page, each beside a photo of the stamp or cover it was written for.

Download the free book (PDF, 13.0 MB, 44 pages)

Free to keep, free to pass along. Print it, keep it on your phone, or share this page with the collector in your family who still reads the packing slip before the stamps.

Inside: Insects and Spiders, the Marvel Super Heroes, Jim Henson and the Muppets, Comic Strip Classics, Alex Trebek, the 1941 China Clipper, an upside-down 1869 Locomotive cover, and thirty-five more.
