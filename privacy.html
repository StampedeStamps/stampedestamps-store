Privacy

The short version: this website asks you for nothing, and StampedeUSA collects no personal information through it. Our web host and our visit counter are described below, with links to their own policies.

Last updated: Monday, November 16, 2026

Who we are

StampedeUSA is a U.S. stamp shop that sells only through its eBay store, ebay.com/str/stampedeusa. Nothing is sold or paid for on this website: every buy button opens the listing on eBay, where the purchase and the payment happen.

To contact us, including with any question or complaint about privacy, send us a message on eBay through our store.

What this website collects

Nothing from you directly. There are no accounts, no sign-up or contact forms and no checkout on this website, so you never give us your name, address, email or payment details here.

The pages of this website set no cookies of their own and do not use your browser's local storage.

Counting visits

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. A small script from Cloudflare on each page reports the page view to Cloudflare, which shows us totals, such as how many visits the site had.

Cloudflare says in its documentation (About Web Analytics) that the service does not collect or use visitors' personal data, and on its Web Analytics product page that it uses no cookies or local storage to collect usage metrics and does not fingerprint individuals for its analytics. See also Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's documentation (What is GitHub Pages?) says that GitHub logs and stores the IP address of every visitor to a GitHub Pages site, for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. See also the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Other websites

Our pages link to eBay, where every purchase happens, and to Facebook and YouTube. Each of those sites has its own privacy policy, which applies when you visit it.

YouTube API Services

StampedeUSA uses YouTube API Services only to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel, StampedeStamps on YouTube. Our upload tool uses only StampedeUSA's own YouTube account, which authorized it, and it does not collect, store or share personal data about YouTube viewers or any other YouTube users.

By using our YouTube channel, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google describes how it handles your information in the Google Privacy Policy.

The access our upload tool has, like any access you have given an app to your own Google account, can be revoked at any time on Google's security settings page.

Changes

We update this page when anything above changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
