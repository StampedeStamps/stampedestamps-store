About this issue

  • IssueAir Post stamp, 1926-27 series
  • Why issuedThe Act of Congress of February 2, 1925, created a rate of 10 cents per ounce for distances to 1000 miles, 15 cents per ounce for 1500 miles and 20 cents for more than 1500 miles on contract air mail routes.

  • Face value20¢
  • Coloryellow green
