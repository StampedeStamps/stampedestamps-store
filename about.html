Every stamp has a story

A stamp is a tiny piece of history you can hold: a president, a moon landing, a flower, a cartoon duck. We love finding those pieces, figuring out exactly what they are, and sending them on to collectors who will enjoy them as much as we do.

That is why our sister site, Stamp Stories, gives every listing its own page and a short story, so you can browse the shelf the way you would flip through an album.

What we promise

  • Exactly as shown

    Offered exactly as shown in the photos. The exact item pictured is what you'll receive, accurately described with no guesswork.

  • Looked up, not guessed

    We identify each stamp from its Scott catalog number, checked against the catalog rather than from memory.

  • Combine and save

    Win or buy more than one? Your shipping drops too, on every multi-item order.

  • Safe checkout

    Every purchase happens on eBay, with eBay's buyer protection behind it.
